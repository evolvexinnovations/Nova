Nova focuses on building the core foundation of the AI Browser by delivering a fast, secure, and production-ready browser experience. The primary goal is to establish a reliable browsing platform with modern browser capabilities before introducing AI-powered features in future releases. Teams collaborate across planning, design, development, testing, and deployment to deliver a complete commercial-grade product that users can adopt as their daily browser. This version lays the technical foundation that every future version will build upon.
